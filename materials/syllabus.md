# CS-1010: Foundations of Computing — Syllabus

## Course Description

CS-1010 introduces the fundamental concepts of computing for students with no prior
programming experience. The course covers the history of computing, how computers
represent and store data, computer hardware, algorithms, programming languages, and
computer networks. Students finish the course able to reason about how computers work
from transistors to the web.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. Describe the major milestones in the history of computing and their significance.
2. Explain how computers represent numbers, text, and images using binary.
3. Identify the main hardware components of a computer and describe their roles.
4. Read and trace simple algorithms expressed in pseudocode.
5. Compare compiled and interpreted programming languages.
6. Explain how data travels across the internet, including packets, IP addresses, and DNS.

## Grading

- Weekly quizzes: 40% (six quizzes, lowest score dropped)
- Midterm project: 25%
- Final exam: 35%

Quizzes are auto-generated from the lesson materials and consist of multiple-choice
questions. Each quiz has five questions. Students may attempt each quiz twice; the
higher score counts.

## Academic Integrity

All quiz answers must reflect the student's own understanding. Students may use the
Study Coach assistant to review material, but not during a quiz attempt.
